How This Works
1
Listen to your activation audio daily
Open the Check-In tab each day and tap your day square to mark it complete. Consistency is everything — every listen installs the new code deeper.
2
Set weekly goals
Each week, write 1–3 action steps that support your transformation. Uncompleted goals carry over automatically to the next week. Review all goals in the Goals tab.
3
Complete your Source Code Daily Journal
Write your daily affirmation, self-love prompts, three gratitudes, and how you felt before and after listening. Your full journal history lives in the Journal tab.
4
Track your progress
The Progress tab shows your activity map, streak, and milestones. When your program is complete, start a new one from Settings — your history is always preserved.
